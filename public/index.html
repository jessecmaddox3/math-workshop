Math Workshop

Two little games.
Lots of ways to think.

Join number tiles to make a target. Find missing values in a rectangle puzzle. Take your time, try another route, and keep your work in view.

Target Number →Use every number once. Combine two chips at a time, see your working, and look for another way.Area Mazes →Seventeen puzzles in three levels. Reason from the labels: the drawings deliberately are not to scale.

Start without an account. Each game saves its own learners in this browser. Use Learners and backups to add a nickname, export progress, or explore optional cloud saves.

Built for my personal use and shared as a starting point. Make it your own, improve mine, or borrow an idea. Cheers!
